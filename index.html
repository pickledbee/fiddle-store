Look inside

Real duets from real fiddle tunes

Hornpipes are short, lively, and reward a steady rhythm and a clean bow, which makes them some of the best ensemble music a young violinist can learn.

Large, easy-to-read music

Each part fills two facing pages, so there is never a page turn in the middle of a tune.

Melody and harmony

A Violin 1 melody part, a Violin 2 harmony part, and a full score for every tune.

First position

Both parts of every tune can be played in first position.

Warm-ups and chord charts

Warm-ups for the hornpipe lilt, scales, double stops, triplets, and dotted rhythms, plus chord charts for guitar, piano, or bass.

For teachers

Group class and recital ideas, a practice tracker, a glossary, and a certificate of achievement.

Binder ready

One-inch margins on both sides, so a three-hole punch or coil binding never cuts into the music.

Twelve tunes in three levels

Level 1: Getting Started

Steady eighth notes, friendly keys, simple chords.
