What level is this music?

Level 1 tunes suit students comfortable with eighth notes in first position. Level 3 tunes add running sixteenths and triplets. Both parts of every tune stay in first position.

How should I print it?

Print at "Actual size" (100%), not "Fit to page." Print double-sided so each part lands on facing pages in a binder. Heavier paper holds up best on a music stand.
