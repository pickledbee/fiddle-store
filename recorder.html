Duet Recorder

Record the melody. Then record the harmony while the melody plays back in your headphones. The page lines up both parts and mixes them into one file.

Wear wired headphones while you record. If the playback comes out of a speaker, the microphone records it too. Bluetooth headphones add a delay, so wired ones line up best.

1. Setup

Tempo, beats per bar, and count-in are locked because a part is recorded. Delete both parts to change them.

Recording delay: not measured
What is the recording delay?

Every phone and computer takes a few milliseconds to play sound and to hear it. The page subtracts that delay so the harmony lands exactly on the melody. To measure it, unplug your headphones, turn the volume up, stay quiet, and tap Measure delay. You will hear eight clicks. Measure once per device. If the parts still sound a little early or late, use the Line up slider on the harmony.
