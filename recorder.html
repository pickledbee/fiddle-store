Duet Recorder

Record the melody. Then record the harmony while the melody plays back in your headphones. Fix any spot, and the page mixes both parts into one file.

Wear wired headphones while you record. If the playback comes out of a speaker, the microphone records it too. Bluetooth headphones add a delay, so wired ones line up best.

1. Setup

Tempo, beats per bar, and count-in are locked because a part is recorded. Delete both parts to change them.

Recording delay: not measured
What is the recording delay?

Every phone and computer takes a few milliseconds to play sound and to hear it. The page subtracts that delay so the harmony lands exactly on the melody. To measure it, unplug your headphones, turn the volume up, stay quiet, and tap Measure delay. You will hear eight clicks. Measure once per device.

How to edit
  1. Choose Melody, Harmony, or Both at the top of this card. Both changes the two parts together, so they stay lined up.
  2. Drag across the waveform to select a spot. Use Zoom in for small spots, and drag the gold edges or tap − and + to adjust the start and end.
  3. Tap Play selection to check that you picked the right notes.
  4. Pick a fix. Re-record this section is best for a wrong note: the music starts a couple of seconds early, the part you are fixing goes quiet at the selection, and you play just that spot. The new notes blend in smoothly.
  5. If you do not like the result, tap Undo last change. You can undo up to 20 changes on each part.
