Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This website, fiddlemusicstore.com, is run by Bruce Bowers, a sole proprietor in Wisconsin. This page explains what information is collected when you visit or buy, and how it is used. If you have questions, email stradman100@gmail.com.

Information collected when you buy

When you buy a PDF, the following is recorded:

  • Your name and email address, as shared by PayPal at checkout or as you type them on this site
  • The state you choose at checkout, and your Wisconsin county if you live in Wisconsin
  • Your order number, what you bought, the amount paid, any sales tax, and how you paid (PayPal or card)

Payments are handled entirely by PayPal. This website never sees or stores your card number, bank details, or PayPal password. PayPal's own privacy policy applies to the information you give PayPal.

How that information is used

  • To email you the PDF you bought and help you if there is a problem with it
  • To calculate and report Wisconsin sales tax as required by law
  • To keep business and tax records

Your information is never sold or rented. It is not used for marketing unless you separately sign up for updates. It is shared only with the services that make the store work (PayPal for payments, and Google for email delivery and record keeping) and with government agencies when required for taxes.

Where it is stored and for how long

Order records are kept in a private Google account that only Bruce Bowers can access. Because sales tax records must be kept for several years, order records are kept for at least four years. If you would like your information removed sooner, email the address above and anything not needed for tax records will be deleted.

Website analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they view, and whether checkout is working. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your general location (city level), device type, browser, and pages visited. It does not tell this site your name or email address. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Amazon affiliate links

Some links on this site go to products on Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, Bruce Bowers earns from qualifying purchases made through those links, at no extra cost to you. When you click one, Amazon may place a cookie in your browser to credit the referral. Amazon's privacy policy applies on Amazon's website.

Children

This store is meant for adults: parents, teachers, and adult players. Children under 13 should not buy or submit personal information on this site. If you believe a child has submitted information, email the address above and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
